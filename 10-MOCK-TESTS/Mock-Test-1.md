# IELTS Mock Test 1 - Tam Test

## 📋 LISTENING - Test 1

### Section 1: Booking a Hotel Room

**Transkript:**

Receptionist: "Good morning, Riverside Hotel. How can I help you?"

Caller: "Hi, I'd like to book a room for next week. I'm looking for something with a good view and preferably near a cafe."

Receptionist: "Certainly! We have several options. When exactly are you planning to visit?"

Caller: "I'm arriving on Tuesday, the 15th, and leaving on Sunday, the 19th."

Receptionist: "So that's four nights. We have a lovely room on the 4th floor with a lake view. It's £120 per night."

Caller: "That sounds good, but is there a cafe nearby? I need my morning coffee!"

Receptionist: "Yes, there's a cafe just next to the hotel entrance. Actually, our hotel guests get a 10% discount there."

Caller: "Excellent! And what about parking? I'm driving."

Receptionist: "We have parking available. It's £15 per day, or you can purchase a weekly pass for £80."

Caller: "I'll take the weekly pass. And one more thing - can I have breakfast included?"

Receptionist: "Our breakfast is an additional £12 per day, or you can add 5 days for £50."

**Sorular:**

1. When is the caller checking in?
   A) Monday 14th
   B) Tuesday 15th
   C) Wednesday 16th

2. What is the nightly room rate?
   A) £100
   B) £110
   C) £120

3. How much does the weekly parking pass cost?
   A) £15
   B) £50
   C) £80

4. What discount do hotel guests get at the nearby cafe?
   A) 5%
   B) 10%
   C) 15%

5. Complete the sentence: The caller will choose the _____________ parking pass.

## 📚 READING - Test 1

### Passage A: Renewable Energy

**Metin:**

"Renewable energy sources, including solar, wind, and hydroelectric power, have become increasingly important in addressing global energy demands whilst minimizing environmental impact. Unlike fossil fuels, which emit greenhouse gases and contribute to climate change, renewable sources generate electricity without producing harmful emissions.

Solar energy, harnessed through photovoltaic cells, has experienced dramatic cost reductions over the past decade. In 2010, solar panels cost approximately $380 per watt; by 2020, this price had fallen to $40 per watt. This 89% reduction has rendered solar energy economically competitive with traditional fossil fuels in many regions.

Wind energy represents another rapidly advancing sector. Modern wind turbines can generate up to 12 megawatts of power, sufficient to supply electricity to approximately 9,000 homes. However, wind farms require substantial land areas and face opposition from communities concerned about noise and visual impact.

Hydroelectric power, the largest producer of renewable electricity globally, provides approximately 16% of the world's electricity. Unlike solar and wind, hydroelectric facilities offer reliable, consistent power generation. However, large-scale dam construction can have significant environmental consequences, disrupting ecosystems and displacing communities.

Despite technological advancements, renewable energy still encounters substantial barriers. Energy storage remains technologically challenging and expensive. Additionally, transitioning from fossil fuels requires significant investment in infrastructure and political will. Nonetheless, declining costs and increasing environmental urgency suggest that renewable energy will comprise an increasingly significant proportion of global energy supply in coming decades."

**Sorular:**

1. According to the passage, what was the primary reason for the reduction in solar panel costs?
   A) Improved government subsidies
   B) Technological advancement and economies of scale
   C) Decreased demand for fossil fuels
   D) Competition from wind energy companies

2. In 2010, solar panels cost approximately:
   A) $40 per watt
   B) $120 per watt
   C) $380 per watt
   D) $400 per watt

3. Complete the sentence: Modern wind turbines can generate up to _____________ megawatts of power.

4. Which of the following is mentioned as an advantage of hydroelectric power?
   A) Low cost of construction
   B) Minimal environmental impact
   C) Consistent, reliable power generation
   D) Easy decommissioning

5. True/False/Not Given: Solar energy is now more expensive than wind energy.
