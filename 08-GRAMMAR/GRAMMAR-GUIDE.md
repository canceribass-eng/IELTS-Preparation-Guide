## Tüm Zamanlar

### PRESENT TENSE (ŞIMDIKI ZAMAN)

#### Simple Present
```
Yapı: Subject + Verb (base form)
"Technology changes society."
"People use smartphones daily."

Kullanım:
- Genel gerçekler
- Alışkanlıklar
- Tekrarlanan eylemler
```

#### Present Continuous
```
Yapı: Subject + Am/Is/Are + Verb-ing
"The population is increasing rapidly."
"Scientists are discovering new species."

Kullanım:
- Şu anda gerçekleşen eylemler
- Geçici durumlar
- Planlanan gelecek (zaman belirteci ile)
```

#### Present Perfect
```
Yapı: Subject + Have/Has + Past Participle
"Technology has revolutionized society."
"Researchers have discovered a cure."

Kullanım:
- Geçmişte başlayan, şimdi devam eden eylemler
- Geçmiş deneyimler (zaman belirtmeden)
- Yakın zamanda tamamlanan eylemler
```

#### Present Perfect Continuous
```
Yapı: Subject + Have/Has + Been + Verb-ing
"Scientists have been studying climate change for decades."
"Pollution has been increasing steadily."

Kullanım:
- Geçmişte başlayan, halen devam eden eylemler (vurgu: devamlılık)
- Ne kadar süredir yapıldığını vurgulama
```

**Band 8-9 Örneği:**
```
"Whilst technological advancement has perpetually 
transformed society, contemporary innovations continue to 
accumulate at an unprecedented pace, fundamentally 
reshaping human interaction."
```

### PAST TENSE (GEÇMIŞ ZAMAN)

#### Simple Past
```
Yapı: Subject + Verb (past form)
"The Industrial Revolution changed society."
"Researchers discovered antibiotics in 1928."

Kullanım:
- Tamamlanan geçmiş eylemler
- Belli bir zaman aralığında gerçekleşen eylemler
- Sıralı geçmiş olaylar
```

#### Past Continuous
```
Yapı: Subject + Was/Were + Verb-ing
"While industrialization was occurring, pollution was increasing."
"Scientists were researching when they made the discovery."

Kullanım:
- Belli bir geçmiş zamanda süren eylemler
- İki eşzamanlı geçmiş eylem
- Araya giren eylem (Simple Past ile)
```

#### Past Perfect
```
Yapı: Subject + Had + Past Participle
"Before the law was passed, damage had accumulated."
"Scientists had analyzed the data before publishing."

Kullanım:
- İki geçmiş eylemden hangisinin önce olduğunu göstermek
- Geçmiş zamanda tamamlanan eylem
```

#### Past Perfect Continuous
```
Yapı: Subject + Had + Been + Verb-ing
"Pollution had been accumulating for centuries before action was taken."
"Researchers had been studying the problem for years."

Kullanım:
- Başka bir geçmiş eylemden önce ne kadar süredir yapıldığını göstermek
```

### FUTURE TENSE (GELECEK ZAMAN)

#### Simple Future
```
Yapı: Subject + Will + Verb (base form)
"Technology will continue to advance."
"Population will increase dramatically."

Kullanım:
- Gelecek tahminleri
- Planlanmamış gelecek kararları
```

#### Future Continuous
```
Yapı: Subject + Will + Be + Verb-ing
"In 2050, people will be living in smart cities."
"Climate change will be affecting agriculture."

Kullanım:
- Gelecek zamanda süren eylemler
- Başka bir gelecek eylemle eşzamanlı olacak eylemler
```

#### Future Perfect
```
Yapı: Subject + Will + Have + Past Participle
"By 2050, scientists will have developed new technologies."
"The population will have doubled by then."

Kullanım:
- Gelecek zamanda tamamlanmış eylemler
- Belirli bir zaman noktasından önce tamamlanacak eylemler
```

#### Future Perfect Continuous
```
Yapı: Subject + Will + Have + Been + Verb-ing
"By 2030, researchers will have been studying this for 20 years."

Kullanım:
- Gelecek zamanda ne kadar süredir yapılacağını göstermek
```

**Band 8-9 Örneği:**
```
"Whilst renewable energy adoption will inevitably accelerate, 
fossil fuels will have dominated for centuries, precipitating 
deleterious environmental consequences that will persist for 
generations."
```

## Kompleks Cümleler

### RELATIVE CLAUSES (İLİŞKİLENDİRME CÜMLELERI)

#### Defining Relative Clauses (Tanımlayıcı)
```
"The technology that revolutionized communication is the internet."
"People who ignore climate change are shortsighted."
"The year when the internet was created was 1989."

Not: Defining clauses için comma yoktur!
```

#### Non-Defining Relative Clauses (Tanımlayıcı Olmayan)
```
"The internet, which revolutionized communication, 
was created in 1989."
"Climate change, which is accelerating rapidly, 
affects ecosystems."

Not: Non-defining clauses için comma vardır!
```

#### Reduced Relative Clauses
```
Normal: "Students who are motivated learn better."
Reduced: "Motivated students learn better."

Normal: "The research that was conducted in 2020 was significant."
Reduced: "The research conducted in 2020 was significant."

Band 8-9'da sıkça kullanılır!
```

### CONDITIONAL SENTENCES (ŞARTLI CÜMLELER)

#### Zero Conditional (Gerçekler)
```
Yapı: If + Simple Present, Simple Present
"If water reaches 100°C, it boils."
"If people exercise regularly, they become healthier."

Kullanım: Genel gerçekler, bilimsel yasalar
```

#### First Conditional (Muhtemel Gelecek)
```
Yapı: If + Simple Present, Will + Verb
"If governments implement stricter policies, pollution will decrease."
"If education improves, society will develop."

Kullanım: Gerçekçi, olası gelecek durumlar
```

#### Second Conditional (Hayali Şimdi/Gelecek)
```
Yapı: If + Simple Past, Would + Verb
"If technology were banned, society would collapse."
"If I were prime minister, I would invest in education."

Kullanım: Hayali, mümkün olmayan durumlar
Not: Were (was yerine) kullanılır!
```

#### Third Conditional (Hayali Geçmiş)
```
Yapı: If + Past Perfect, Would Have + Past Participle
"If governments had acted earlier, climate change would have been less severe."
"If the discovery hadn't been made, technology wouldn't have advanced."

Kullanım: Geçmişte yapılmamış şeylerin hayali sonuçları
```

#### Mixed Conditional
```
"If scientists had researched this earlier (Geçmiş), 
we would know the answer now (Şimdi)."

"If she hadn't studied hard (Geçmiş), 
she wouldn't be successful now (Şimdi)."
```

**Band 8-9 Örneği:**
```
"Had governments implemented stringent environmental policies 
decades ago, we would not be confronting the existential 
threat of climate catastrophe today."
```

## Pasif Ses

### Basit Pasif Yapılar

```
Aktif: Subject + Verb + Object
"Researchers discovered a cure."

Pasif: Object + Be + Past Participle + By + Subject
"A cure was discovered by researchers."
```

### Zamanlara Göre Pasif

```
Simple Present Passive: Is/Are + Past Participle
"Technology is used worldwide."

Present Continuous Passive: Is/Are + Being + Past Participle
"New policies are being implemented."

Simple Past Passive: Was/Were + Past Participle
"The theory was disproven."

Present Perfect Passive: Has/Have + Been + Past Participle
"Solutions have been found."
```

### Akademik Pasif (Band 8-9)

```
"It has been argued that..."
"It can be seen that..."
"It is widely believed that..."
"It could be suggested that..."
"It is evident that..."
"It remains to be determined whether..."
```

**Band 8-9 Örneği:**
```
"Whilst the mechanisms by which climate change precipitates 
ecological devastation have been extensively elucidated, 
implementing remedial interventions remains fraught with 
political obstacles."
```

## Sık Yapılan Hatalar

### HATA 1: Subject-Verb Uyuşmazlığı

```
❌ YANLIŞ: "The data show a clear trend, but each result are different."
✅ DOĞRU: "The data show a clear trend, but each result is different."

Kural: "Each", "every", "either", "neither" = Singular verb
```

### HATA 2: Article Kullanımı

```
❌ YANLIŞ: "Technology is important tool in modern society."
✅ DOĞRU: "Technology is an important tool in modern society."

❌ YANLIŞ: "The climate change is the biggest problem."
✅ DOĞRU: "Climate change is the biggest problem."

Kural:
- Sayılabilir tekil = a/an
- Sayılamaz isimler = article yok
```

### HATA 3: Tense Consistency

```
❌ YANLIŞ: "Research showed that students who study regularly 
perform better on tests."
✅ DOĞRU: "Research shows that students who study regularly 
perform better on tests."

Kural: Genel gerçekler = Simple Present
```

### HATA 4: Parallelism (Paralellik)

```
❌ YANLIŞ: "Education develops creativity, improves skills, 
and students gain confidence."
✅ DOĞRU: "Education develops creativity, improves skills, 
and builds confidence."

Kural: Liste öğeleri aynı yapıda olmalı
```

### HATA 5: Word Order

```
❌ YANLIŞ: "Only a few people the solution understand."
✅ DOĞRU: "Only a few people understand the solution."

Kural: SVO (Subject-Verb-Object) sırası
```

### HATA 6: Dangling Modifiers

```
❌ YANLIŞ: "After analyzing the data, the conclusion was clear."
✅ DOĞRU: "After analyzing the data, researchers reached a clear conclusion."

Kural: Modifier'ın subject'i açık olmalı
```

### HATA 7: Run-on Sentences

```
❌ YANLIŞ: "Technology has advanced, society has changed, 
people are more connected."
✅ DOĞRU: "Technology has advanced, society has changed, 
and people are more connected."

Kural: Bağlaç veya noktalama ile birleştir
```

### HATA 8: Preposition Yanlışlığı

```
❌ YANLIŞ: "The solution is based to research."
✅ DOĞRU: "The solution is based on research."

❌ YANLIŞ: "The impact in education is significant."
✅ DOĞRU: "The impact on education is significant."

Sık Prepositions:
- Based on, focused on, impact on
- Concerned with, familiar with, consistent with
- Result in, lead to, contribute to
```

## Band 8-9 Dilbilgisi Teknikleri

### 1. Inversion (Ters Çevirme)

```
Normal: "I have rarely seen such innovation."
Inverted: "Rarely have I seen such innovation."

Normal: "Not only does technology help, but it also creates problems."
Inverted: "Not only does technology help, but it also creates problems."

Sık Kullanılan Adverbs:
- Rarely, Seldom, Scarcely, Hardly ever
- Never, Only then, Only after
- No sooner, Under no circumstances
```

### 2. Cleft Sentences

```
Normal: "Government policy caused the economic crisis."
Cleft: "It was government policy that caused the economic crisis."

Normal: "Environmental degradation threatens biodiversity."
Cleft: "What threatens biodiversity is environmental degradation."
```

### 3. Nominalizations (İsimleştirme)

```
Verb → Noun (Akademik tonda sıkça kullanılır)
"Society developed" → "Social development"
"Pollution increased" → "Increasing pollution"
"Technology advanced" → "Technological advancement"

Band 8-9: "The proliferation of digital technology precipitated 
unforeseen consequences."
```

### 4. Complex Noun Phrases

```
Simple: "The problem is difficult."
Complex: "The multifaceted nature of this complex socioeconomic 
challenge."

Band 8-9: "The confluence of technological innovation, 
environmental degradation, and societal transformation..."
```
