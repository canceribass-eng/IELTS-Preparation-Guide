## SORU 1: Multiple Choice (Çoktan Seçmeli)

### Gerçek Soru

**Pasaj:**

"The development of artificial intelligence has revolutionized many industries. Machine learning algorithms can now analyze vast datasets and identify patterns that would be impossible for humans to detect manually. However, this technological advancement comes with significant challenges, particularly regarding data privacy and algorithmic bias. Companies must balance innovation with ethical considerations."

**Soru:** According to the passage, one challenge of artificial intelligence development is:

A) The high cost of computer hardware
B) Difficulty in analyzing data patterns
C) Data privacy and algorithmic bias concerns
D) Lack of human involvement in the process

---

### Cevap Analizi ve Puanlama

#### ❌ DÜŞÜK PUAN (3-4 puan) - Yanlış Cevap
**Seçilen cevap: B**

**Analiz:**
- Soruyu yanlış anlamış
- Pasajda "algorithms CAN analyze" yazıyor, problem değil
- Dikkat eksikliği
- Puanlama: 0 (yanlış cevap)

---

#### 🟡 ORTA PUAN (5-6 puan) - Kısmen Doğru Mantık
**Seçilen cevap: A**

**Analış:**
- Harita dışı bilgi kullanmış
- Pasajda hardware maliyeti belirtilmemiş
- Metin içindeki detayları kaçırmış
- Puanlama: 0 (yanlış cevap)

---

#### ✅ YÜKSEK PUAN (7-9 puan) - Doğru Cevap
**Seçilen cevap: C**

**Neden doğru?**
- "significant challenges, particularly regarding data privacy and algorithmic bias" metinde açıkça yazılı
- Metin dikkatle okunmuş
- Doğru cevap seçilmiş
- Puanlama: 1 (doğru cevap)

## SORU 2: True/False/Not Given

### Gerçek Soru

**Pasaj:**

"Renewable energy sources such as solar and wind power have become increasingly important in reducing carbon emissions. Solar panels have become 80% cheaper in the past decade. Many countries have set ambitious targets for renewable energy adoption. However, storage technology remains a significant barrier to widespread implementation."

**Sorular:**
1. Solar panels are the most efficient energy source available.
   - True / False / Not Given

2. Solar panel prices have decreased significantly in recent years.
   - True / False / Not Given

3. Storage technology is a challenge for renewable energy implementation.
   - True / False / Not Given

---

### Cevap Analizi

#### Soru 1: "Solar panels are the most efficient energy source available."

**❌ DÜŞÜK PUAN Cevabı: TRUE**
- Pasajda verimlilik bahsedilmemiş
- Sadece fiyat düşüşü yazılı
- Sonuç çıkarma hatasıdır
- Puanlama: 0

**✅ YÜKSEK PUAN Cevabı: NOT GIVEN**
- Pasaj verimlilikten bahsetmiyor
- Sadece fiyatlandırmayı tartışıyor
- Doğru mantık yürütme
- Puanlama: 1

#### Soru 2: "Solar panel prices have decreased significantly in recent years."

**✅ YÜKSEK PUAN Cevabı: TRUE**
- "80% cheaper in the past decade" - pasajda açık
- Doğru bilgi taraması
- Puanlama: 1

#### Soru 3: "Storage technology is a challenge for renewable energy implementation."

**✅ YÜKSEK PUAN Cevabı: TRUE**
- "storage technology remains a significant barrier" - pasajda yazılı
- Doğru anlama
- Puanlama: 1

## SORU 3: Matching Headings (Başlıkları Eşleştirme)

### Gerçek Soru

**Pasajlar:**

**Paragraph A:**
"The human brain contains approximately 86 billion neurons. Each neuron can connect with thousands of other neurons, forming an incredibly complex network. This interconnectedness is what enables learning, memory formation, and higher cognitive functions. Scientists estimate that the brain makes about 100 trillion connections."

**Paragraph B:**
"Meditation has been shown to increase gray matter density in specific brain regions associated with attention and emotional regulation. Regular practitioners show improved focus and reduced anxiety levels. Brain imaging studies reveal physical changes in the structure of meditators' brains after just eight weeks of practice."

**Paragraph C:**
"Neurodegenerative diseases like Alzheimer's affect the structure and function of neurons, leading to cognitive decline. Currently, there is no cure for these diseases, but early detection through brain imaging and cognitive tests can help manage symptoms and potentially slow progression."

**Başlık Seçenekleri:**
i) The therapeutic effects of mindfulness on brain structure
ii) The complexity of neural networks
iii) Treatment options for brain diseases
iv) Why neurons are important for learning
v) Detecting early signs of neurological disorders

---

### Cevap Analizi

**❌ DÜŞÜK PUAN Eşleştirmesi:**
- Paragraph A → iii) Treatment options for brain diseases
- Paragraph B → iv) Why neurons are important for learning
- Paragraph C → i) The therapeutic effects of mindfulness

**Neden yanlış?**
- Pasaj A yapıyı anlatıyor, tedavi değil
- Pasaj B meditasyonu anlatıyor, nöron önemini değil
- Pasaj C hastalıkları anlatıyor, meditasyonu değil
- Dikkat eksikliği: metni yüzeysel okumuş
- Puanlama: 0/3

---

**✅ YÜKSEK PUAN Eşleştirmesi:**
- Paragraph A → ii) The complexity of neural networks
- Paragraph B → i) The therapeutic effects of mindfulness on brain structure
- Paragraph C → v) Detecting early signs of neurological disorders

**Neden doğru?**
- Pasaj A: "interconnectedness", "100 trillion connections" → komplekslik hakkında
- Pasaj B: "increase gray matter density", "physical changes in brain structure" → meditasyonun beyin yapısı üzerindeki etkileri
- Pasaj C: "early detection through brain imaging and cognitive tests" → erken teşhis hakkında
- Metin dikkatle analiz edilmiş
- Her pasajın ana fikrini doğru anlamış
- Puanlama: 3/3

## SORU 4: Completion - Şıkları Tamamlama

### Gerçek Soru

**Pasaj:**

"Climate change poses unprecedented challenges to global food security. Rising temperatures affect crop yields, while extreme weather events like droughts and floods destroy harvests. Additionally, changing precipitation patterns disrupt water availability for irrigation. Small-scale farmers in developing countries are particularly vulnerable because they lack resources for adaptation technologies. International cooperation and investment in sustainable agricultural practices are essential to address this crisis."

**Sorular:**

1. According to the passage, crop yields are affected by ___________

A) Poor farming techniques
B) Rising temperatures
C) Lack of seeds
D) Population growth

2. Small-scale farmers are particularly vulnerable because they ___________

A) Live in tropical regions
B) Don't use modern equipment
C) Lack resources for adaptation technologies
D) Don't understand climate science

---

### Cevap Analizi

**Soru 1:**

❌ **DÜŞÜK PUAN: A) Poor farming techniques**
- Pasajda farming technique bahsedilmemiş
- Hızlı okuma sonucu yanlış seçim
- Puanlama: 0

✅ **YÜKSEK PUAN: B) Rising temperatures**
- "Rising temperatures affect crop yields" - doğrudan pasajda yazılı
- Metin dikkatle okunmuş
- Puanlama: 1

**Soru 2:**

❌ **DÜŞÜK PUAN: B) Don't use modern equipment**
- Pasajda equipment bahsedilmemiş
- Varsayım yapılmış
- Puanlama: 0

✅ **YÜKSEK PUAN: C) Lack resources for adaptation technologies**
- "they lack resources for adaptation technologies" - pasajda tam metniyle yazılı
- Doğru bilgi çıkarımı
- Puanlama: 1
