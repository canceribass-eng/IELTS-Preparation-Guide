## SORU 1: Multiple Choice - Section 1

### Gerçek Soru

**Transkript:**

**Person A:** "Hi, I'd like to book a fitness class, please."

**Person B:** "Of course! Which class are you interested in? We offer yoga, pilates, spinning, and boxing."

**Person A:** "I'm looking for something that helps with flexibility and stress relief. I've been really tense lately."

**Person B:** "Perfect! I'd recommend either yoga or pilates. Yoga focuses more on mindfulness and breathing, while pilates is great for core strength and flexibility."

**Person A:** "Okay, I think I'll go with yoga. How many classes per week do you offer?"

**Person B:** "We have classes Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 6 PM, and Saturday morning at 9 AM."

**Person A:** "Great! And what's the cost?"

**Person B:** "It's £15 per class, or £50 for a package of four classes, which saves you £10."

---

**Soru:** Why does Person A want to take a fitness class?

A) To build muscle strength
B) For flexibility and stress relief
C) To prepare for a competition
D) Because their doctor recommended it

---

### ❌ DÜŞÜK PUAN CEVABI (Band 3-4)

**Seçilen cevap: A) To build muscle strength**

**Analiz:**
- Dinleme sırasında dikkat dağılmış
- "core strength" kelimeleri kapalı kapı yaptı
- Gerçek neden (flexibility and stress relief) gözden kaçırıldı
- Puanlama: 0 (yanlış cevap)

**Hata Türü:** Yarım dinleme, çerçeve oluşturma hatası

---

### 🟡 ORTA PUAN CEVABI (Band 5-6)

**Seçilen cevap: B) For flexibility and stress relief**

**Analiz:**
- Doğru cevap seçilmiş
- "flexibility and stress relief" ifadesi açıkça duyulmuş
- Ancak neden Person A'nın yoga'yı pilates'ten seçtiğini tam olarak anlamamış olabilir
- Puanlama: 1 (doğru cevap)

---

### ✅ YÜKSEK PUAN CEVABI (Band 8-9)

**Seçilen cevap: B) For flexibility and stress relief**

**Neden yüksek puan?**
- "I'm looking for something that helps with flexibility and stress relief. I've been really tense lately." - kesin ve açık cevap
- Dinleme becerisi mükemmel
- Bağlam anlayışı tam
- Puanlama: 1 (doğru cevap + tam anlama)

## SORU 3: Multiple Answer - Section 2

### Gerçek Soru

**Transkript:**

"Welcome to the Environmental Conservation Organization. I'm here to tell you about our recent initiatives. This year, we've launched several programs to protect endangered species. First, we've established a wildlife sanctuary in the Amazon rainforest where jaguars and pink river dolphins are protected. Second, we're running an ocean cleanup program focused on reducing plastic waste in coastal areas. Third, we've partnered with local communities to create sustainable farming practices. Additionally, we've developed an educational program for schools to raise awareness about climate change. Finally, we're funding research into renewable energy technologies."

**Soru:** Which of the following are mentioned as initiatives by the organization? (Select 3)

A) Protecting endangered animals in a wildlife sanctuary
B) Building new schools in rural areas
C) Organizing ocean cleanup programs
D) Developing educational programs about climate change
E) Creating sustainable farming practices
F) Banning all fishing in coastal waters

---

### ❌ DÜŞÜK PUAN CEVAPLARI (Band 3-4)

**Seçilen: B, C, F**

- B seçilmiş - metinde yok (yanlış)
- C seçilmiş - doğru
- F seçilmiş - metinde yok (yanlış)

**Puanlama: 1/3**

---

### 🟡 ORTA PUAN CEVAPLARI (Band 6-6.5)

**Seçilen: A, C, D**

- A seçilmiş - "established a wildlife sanctuary" ✓
- C seçilmiş - "ocean cleanup program" ✓
- D seçilmiş - "educational program for schools" ✓

**Puanlama: 3/3** ✓

---

### ✅ YÜKSEK PUAN CEVAPLARI (Band 8-9)

**Seçilen: A, C, D** + **Açıklama:**

"According to the transcript:
- A is correct: 'established a wildlife sanctuary in the Amazon rainforest'
- C is correct: 'running an ocean cleanup program focused on reducing plastic waste'
- D is correct: 'developed an educational program for schools to raise awareness about climate change'
- B is incorrect: Schools are not mentioned
- E is mentioned but not selected: 'partnered with local communities to create sustainable farming practices' - This could be debated but educational awareness was more specific
- F is incorrect: Only cleanup, not banning fishing"

**Puanlama: 3/3 + Full Understanding**

## 🎯 Dinleme Stratejileri

### Yüksek Puan Almak İçin:
1. ✓ Soruları önceden okuyun (hızlı tarama)
2. ✓ Anahtar kelimeleri vurgulayın
3. ✓ İlk geçişte cevapları yazın
4. ✓ Harfleme, sayılar ve isimler dikkat edin
5. ✓ Sonu dinlemeyi ihmal etmeyin
6. ✓ Pauses kullanarak yazma imkanı bulun
7. ✓ Yardımcı kelimeler dinleyin ("first", "however", "finally")
8. ✓ Redaksiyon zamanında cevapları kontrol edin

### Sık Yapılan Hatalar:
- ✗ Soruları önceden okumamak
- ✗ Bir kelimeleri kaçırdığında çöküş
- ✗ Ortam gürültüsünü bahane göstermek
- ✗ Hızlı konuşmayı takip edememe
- ✗ Yazım hataları (isimler, sayılar)
- ✗ Konuşmacıların tonunu görmezden gelme
- ✗ İlk cevaplarını ikinci dinlemede değiştirme (genelde hatalı)
